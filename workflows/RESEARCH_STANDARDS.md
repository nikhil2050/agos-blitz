# Research Standards
Define question, decision, geography, segment and period. Prefer primary sources: official product/pricing pages, filings, government/regulatory sources, technical docs and direct customer evidence. Record title, URL, publisher, publication/access date, evidence type, scope and confidence. Separate fact, estimate, assumption, hypothesis and opinion. Do not invent TAM/SAM/SOM, pricing, revenue, market share or user counts. Market sizing must show bottom-up inputs and formulas. Compare direct competitors, indirect alternatives and status quo. Desk research is not customer validation. Never invent interviews, quotes, pilots or purchase intent.
