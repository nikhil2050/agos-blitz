# Founder-led Technology Decisions
Technology is not fixed by this template. Derive needs from approved requirements, then ask about founder skills/preferences, budget, hosting, timeline, integrations, compliance and expected scale. Present 2–3 viable options where useful. Compare requirement fit, learning curve, delivery speed, ecosystem, cost, operations, security, portability, lock-in and maintainability. Give a clearly labelled recommendation and trade-offs. The founder selects or requests more research. Record alternatives, rationale, consequences and date in `docs/TECHNOLOGY_DECISIONS.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Never impose a stack from another project, choose fashion over fit, silently add paid services or make irreversible vendor/data decisions without approval.
