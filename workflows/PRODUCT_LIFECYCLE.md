# Product Lifecycle and Gates
Founder navigates the steps. Agent recommends the next step and waits for approval at gates.

0. **Setup:** read brief; select agent, project type, profile, research expectations and technology decision process. Output `docs/PROJECT_CONFIGURATION.md` and initial `docs/AI_CONTEXT.md`. Gate: confirm working mode.
1. **Discovery:** identify customer, problem, trigger, alternatives, urgency, geography and value hypothesis. Output product vision and assumptions/risks. Gate: approve problem framing.
2. **Business analysis:** research market, competitors, differentiation, validation, revenue model, pricing, cost drivers and go-to-market. Output business case, research and validation plan. Gate: proceed, validate, pivot or pause.
3. **Product definition:** define journeys, actors, MVP/non-goals, requirements, priorities, permissions, edge cases and acceptance criteria. Output PRD, journeys and roadmap. Gate: approve scope.
4. **UX/UI:** define information architecture, screens, interaction patterns, visual direction, responsive behaviour and accessibility. Gate: approve experience.
5. **Technology/architecture:** compare options with founder; define architecture, data, APIs, security, privacy, hosting and operations. Gate: approve technical direction.
6. **Delivery planning:** break work into milestones/tasks with dependencies, acceptance criteria and test strategy. Gate: approve implementation plan.
7. **Implementation:** inspect, plan, build incrementally, test, review security and update docs. Gate: review milestones and scope changes.
8. **Release readiness:** test quality, security, accessibility, performance, backups, monitoring, rollback and operational readiness. Gate: founder authorizes release.
9. **Launch/learning:** positioning, onboarding, acquisition, support, analytics, feedback and iteration.

Founder may skip/combine phases explicitly. Explain consequences and record the choice. Never skip essential security, privacy or regulatory checks merely to save time.
