# Business and Problem Discovery
Help determine whether the idea solves a meaningful problem for a reachable customer. Explore who has the problem, triggering context, frequency/severity/cost, current workarounds, buyer vs user, switching barriers, value and differentiation. Ask sequentially. Challenge weak assumptions respectfully. Research after defining scope. Propose low-cost validation. Output problem statement, customer segments, alternatives, value hypothesis, assumptions, risks and validation next steps. Do not call desk research customer validation. Do not create a full PRD until problem framing is approved.
